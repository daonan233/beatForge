# Hit sound assets

The hit sounds are edited from real acoustic drum-kit cymbal recordings published by BigSoundBank / LaSonotheque:

- `hit-tap.ogg`: 140 ms high-passed, bright edit of **Hi-hat close #5** (`2300.ogg`)
- `hit-hold-head.ogg`: 300 ms high-passed edit of **Cymbale 16\" #7** (`2317.ogg`)
- `hit-hold-tail.ogg`: 100 ms extra-bright edit of **Hi-hat close #5** (`2300.ogg`)

Sources:

- <https://bigsoundbank.com/cymbale-16-7-s2317.html>
- <https://bigsoundbank.com/hi-hat-close-5-s2300.html>

The source pages release both recordings under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They may be used, modified, and distributed in personal and commercial projects without attribution.

The shipped OGG files were trimmed, high-pass filtered, treble-boosted, faded, and gain-adjusted for dense rhythm-game playback. They contain no assets extracted from BanG Dream! or another commercial game.
